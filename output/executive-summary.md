# Executive Summary: Data Governance & Risk Mitigation

## Objective
To audit, cleanse, and optimize the Assembly’s data environment, ensuring readiness for AI-assisted workflows. This project establishes the baseline data integrity required to mitigate PII exposure and streamline departmental asset management.

## Key Strategic Findings
*   **Data Integrity & Security**: Identified and neutralized duplicate and orphan records within the PII detection set. By cleaning these inputs, we have drastically reduced the risk of unauthorized data exposure in AI-indexed environments.
*   **Operational Efficiency**: Conducted a comprehensive audit of licensing and departmental naming conventions. This effort eliminates redundant license costs (E7/E3 optimization) and resolves the "naming drift" that currently hampers cross-departmental reporting.
*   **Risk Standardization**: Transitioned disparate date formats and site inventory records into a standardized ISO 8601 framework. This ensures that all future automated reporting and Copilot-indexing are based on a singular, reliable source of truth.

## Strategic Impact
By shifting from reactive data management to a proactive governance framework, the Assembly can now leverage its data as a reliable asset rather than a liability. These improvements not only satisfy immediate compliance requirements but create a scalable foundation for future digital transformation initiatives.
