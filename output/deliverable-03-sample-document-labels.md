# Sample-Document Labeling

| Document | Recommended Label | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| leadership_pack_draft.txt | Restricted | Contains highly sensitive, non-public information including in-camera committee proceedings and unannounced senior staffing changes. |
| media_release_approved.txt | Public | Explicitly cleared for public release on the Assembly website and contains no confidential or sensitive data. |
| procurement_card_review.txt | Internal | Represents standard internal operational reporting and audit findings that are intended for Assembly staff, but do not meet the "Restricted" threshold. |
